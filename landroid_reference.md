# EXPLORER NOTICE

Welcome, Explorer.

You have been assigned to the **Microversal Anomalies Research
Programme**, an ongoing interuniversal exploration initiative operated
by the Kartik's Aeronautics and Space Agency (KASA).

Your assignment involves the exploration, observation and documentation
of small, self-contained universes exhibiting unusual temporal and
spatial behaviour.

You will be operating a **KASA IR-002S**, an Interadion Travel Ship
developed specifically for exploration across otherwise inaccessible
reality realms.

This archive contains information necessary for your assignment. You are
expected to familiarise yourself with its contents before commencing
exploration.

Remember: You are a representative of KASA. All observations,
discoveries and collected data are to be treated as agency property.

# 01 // THE ANOMALIES

### 1.1 --- General Information

The Interuniversal Research Division has identified a collection of
previously undocumented spatial anomalies.

These anomalies appear to contain small, self-contained universes, each
possessing its own celestial system.

Current observations indicate that these universes generally contain:

-   A single central star.
-   A varying number of orbiting planets.
-   Independent celestial characteristics.
-   Distinct planetary environments and properties.

Despite extensive research, the origin, nature and underlying mechanism
of these anomalies remain unknown.

### 1.2 --- Temporal Behaviour

One of the most unusual characteristics of these anomalies is their
relationship with time.

The spatial locations at which these universes can be accessed are not
constant.

Observations indicate that:

-   A new micro-universe becomes accessible with each passing Earth day
    in UNIVERSE_00.
-   Each daily anomaly is associated with its corresponding Earth date.
-   The accessible locations change over time.
-   Previously accessible anomalies may be revisited through temporal
    displacement.

The reason behind this behaviour remains undetermined.

KASA has not established whether these anomalies are naturally
occurring, artificially generated or the result of an unknown
cosmological phenomenon.

### 1.3 --- Unscheduled Anomalies

Not all micro-universes are associated with an Earth date.

Certain anomalies can be accessed without a corresponding temporal
designation.

Their accessibility is made possible through the combined operation of
the IR-002S temporal displacement and teleportation systems.

This allows explorers to access a wider range of environments beyond
those available through the standard date-associated selection process.

The underlying reason for their existence and accessibility remains
under investigation.

# 02 // KASA CRAFT IR-002S

### 2.1 --- Overview

The **IR-002S** is a specialised vessel belonging to KASA's **Interadion
Travel Ship (IR) series**.

The term *Interadion* derives from *inter-realmonical*, referring to
travel between separate reality realms.

The designation 002S identifies its specific model.

These vessels are designed to facilitate travel across reality realms
and explore otherwise inaccessible microversal environments.

The craft incorporates advanced systems that allow it to operate
independently within unfamiliar celestial systems.

### 2.2 --- Xytranium Hull

The exterior structure of the IR-002S is constructed primarily from
**XYTRANIUM**.

Xytranium is an exceptionally durable, artificially manufactured
supermetal.

It is named after its founder, Xytro.

Unlike naturally occurring metals, Xytranium is entirely synthetic,
having been developed through artificial manufacturing processes.

Its exceptional structural durability makes it suitable for the
demanding conditions encountered during interuniversal exploration.

### 2.3 --- Temporal Displacement System

The IR-002S contains an integrated time machine.

Its primary function is to transport the craft to the appropriate
temporal position associated with a selected microversal anomaly.

This system allows explorers to access date-associated universes
corresponding to specific Earth dates in UNIVERSE_00.

It also permits the revisitation of previously accessible anomalies.

### 2.4 --- Interuniversal Teleportation System

The craft is equipped with an integrated teleportation machine.

When operated alongside the time machine, it enables access to
micro-universes without an associated Earth date.

This allows explorers to access a wider range of environments beyond
those available through the standard date-associated selection process.

### 2.5 --- System Integration

Both systems operate as part of a unified navigation architecture.

The time machine establishes the required temporal position, while the
teleportation machine facilitates access to the selected universe.

**WARNING:** The underlying mechanisms responsible for the changing
spatial distribution of microversal anomalies remain unknown.

# 03 // EXPLORATION PERSONNEL

### 3.1 --- Personnel Assignment

All explorers assigned to the Microversal Anomalies Research Programme
are employees of KASA.

Exploration assignments are issued internally by the agency.

Personnel are expected to operate within the scope of their assigned
duties and follow the procedures outlined in this archive.

### 3.2 --- Responsibilities

Every assigned explorer is responsible for:

-   Operating their designated IR-series craft.
-   Observing and documenting encountered celestial systems.
-   Investigating accessible planetary environments.
-   Recording unusual phenomena.
-   Submitting collected observations to the Interuniversal Research
    Division.

Explorers are reminded that even seemingly insignificant observations
may prove valuable to ongoing research.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 04 // EXPLORATION PROCEDURES

### 4.1 --- Arrival

Upon successful arrival, explorers should familiarise themselves with
the surrounding celestial environment.

Each system may differ in stellar characteristics, planetary count,
orbital configuration and other observable properties.

Do not assume that two systems will exhibit identical characteristics.

### 4.2 --- Stellar Observation

The central star is the primary reference point for identifying and
studying the surrounding system.

Record any unusual stellar characteristics or observable phenomena.

### 4.3 --- Planetary Exploration

Explorers are encouraged to approach and land on individual planets
whenever possible.

Planetary exploration is essential to the programme's research
objectives.

Record notable environmental characteristics and any unusual
observations encountered during landing operations.

### 4.4 --- Exploration Records

All observations should be treated as potentially significant.

Even seemingly insignificant differences between two micro-universes may
provide useful information regarding the nature of these anomalies.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 05 // RESEARCH DIRECTIVES

The Interuniversal Research Division currently operates under the
following objectives:

1.  **Identification:** Locate and catalogue accessible micro-universes.
2.  **Observation:** Document stellar and planetary characteristics.
3.  **Exploration:** Investigate individual planets and their
    environments.
4.  **Comparison:** Identify similarities and differences between
    separate anomalies.
5.  **Temporal Research:** Investigate the relationship between Earth
    dates and anomaly accessibility.
6.  **Origin Research:** Determine the underlying cause of these
    phenomena.

The sixth objective remains particularly challenging.

No conclusive explanation has been established.
